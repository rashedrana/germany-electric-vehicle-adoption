Structured & unstructured data

Structured data

A fully structured annual panel: two source series — EV sales and EV share of new cars — from Our World in Data (based on the IEA Global EV Outlook), keyed by country and year, plus the paper’s 2022 income cross-section. Wide and long shapes of the same data are kept side by side, as in Projects 1 and 2.

Unstructured data

Fully structured source — no free text. The OWID/IEA panel carries numeric series only; there is no unstructured content to process.

How the data was cleaned

  • Derived columns are computed in data prep exactly as in the paper, and labelled derivations, not source data: cumulative sales (running sum — the fleet proxy behind the 15-million target), implied total market (EV sales ÷ EV share) and YoY growth.
  • Coverage gaps are real source gaps, kept as missing rows: a few small markets enter the OWID panel after 2010, and 6 entity-years carry sales without a published share, so no implied market is computed for them.
  • The source carries no BEV/PHEV split — “EV” means battery-electric and plug-in hybrid combined, and no chart pretends otherwise.
  • Total fact rows: 2,999. The panel is intrinsically smaller than Projects 1–2 (16 years × 64 entities is everything the source holds) — the count is stated openly, and no rows are padded or interpolated.
